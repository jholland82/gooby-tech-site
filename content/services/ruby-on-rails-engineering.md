## Product Velocity Meets Architectural Discipline

Ruby on Rails has powered many of the world's most successful tech companies. Its convention-over-configuration philosophy enables small, senior engineering teams to outpace organizations five times their size.

At **Gooby Technologies, LLC**, we combine the rapid iteration speed of Rails with the engineering rigor required for enterprise scale. We write idiomatic, clean Ruby code, enforce strict domain boundaries, and optimize database access so your application never outgrows its architecture.

### What We Deliver

- **Greenfield Rails Platforms**: Complete application architecture from domain modeling and database schema design to production deployment.
- **Hotwire & Turbo Reactivity**: Build fast, single-page application experiences using HTML-over-the-wire without the maintenance burden of client-side JavaScript state synchronization.
- **High-Throughput Sidekiq Workflows**: Reliable background processing for billing runs, transactional emails, data synchronization, and third-party API webhooks.
- **ActiveRecord Tuning**: Eliminating N+1 queries, optimizing complex joins, implementing partial database indexes, and configuring connection pool sizes for high concurrency.
