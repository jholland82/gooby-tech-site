## Strategic Technical Leadership on Demand

Technical missteps in the early stages of a software venture can cost months of wasted runway. Choosing the wrong framework, mismanaging technical debt, or hiring the wrong team profile can derail a company's momentum.

A **Fractional CTO from Gooby Tech** provides the seasoned judgment of a veteran engineering executive. We help non-technical founders navigate technical decisions, mentor growing engineering teams, and prepare your architecture for rapid enterprise scaling.

### Where We Add Immediate Value

- **Technology Stack Selection**: Selecting languages, cloud providers, and third-party vendors based on total cost of ownership and developer velocity.
- **Hiring & Team Building**: Formulating technical interview processes, evaluating senior candidate code submissions, and building high-performance engineering cultures.
- **Investor & Due Diligence Readiness**: Assisting founders through technical due diligence for Series A/B venture capital rounds or M&A evaluations.
- **Engineering Process & Cadence**: Instituting agile practices that actually work: lightweight RFCs, effective async standups, and continuous deployment habits.
